THE INSTITUTE PHILOSOPHY

Where Tactile Craft Meets Editorial Vision

Great fashion is never merely decorative—it is architecture for the human form. At Fashion Designing Institute, students learn from day one by working directly with muslin, silk, wool, and natural fibers on studio dress forms rather than relying solely on theory.

Our curriculum pairs intimate 1:10 atelier mentorship with modern CLO 3D simulation and annual runway juries, ensuring every graduate leaves with both refined handcraft and a commanding commercial portfolio.

"Every stitch is a decision. When students master pattern geometry and fabric behavior, their imagination has no ceiling."

— Prof. Elena Vance & Julian Moreau, Founding Directors

FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Admissions & Studio FAQ

Everything prospective students ask about portfolios, equipment, and scholarships

Do I need prior sewing experience to apply?
No prior industrial sewing experience is required for our Foundation and B.Des tracks. We teach pattern drafting, machine control, and draping from first principles during your opening studio semester.
What should I include in my admissions portfolio?
Submit 8 to 12 pieces showcasing your creative eye—this can include fashion sketches, moodboards, photography, handmade garments, textile experiments, or digital illustrations.
Are sewing machines and dress forms provided on campus?
Yes. Every studio wing is equipped with industrial Juki lockstitch and overlock machines, steaming stations, cutting tables, and calibrated dress forms included in your studio fee.
Can I upload my portfolio and register for shows online?
Yes! Once registered, your Student Dashboard gives you direct access to course modules, portfolio image uploads, faculty messaging, and runway workshop registration.
