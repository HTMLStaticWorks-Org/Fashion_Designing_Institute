Admissions & Portfolio Guide

Your Complete Guide to Joining the Atelier

Step-by-step portfolio preparation, studio orientation, and faculty interview tips for incoming design students.

Step-By-Step Preparation

How to Prepare Your Design Application

1

Select Your Design Program

Choose between our 3-year B.Des Fashion Design degree, 1-year Atelier Diploma, or 6-month specialized certificates.

2

Curate 8–12 Creative Works

Include fashion sketches, moodboards, draped garments, textile experiments, or editorial photography showing your creative voice.

3

Upload via Student Portal

Register your account and preview your portfolio plates inside the Student Dashboard for faculty jury feedback.

4

Studio Orientation & Kit Pickup

Receive your drafting kit, dress form assignment, and access to our industrial sewing & textile labs.

Atelier Standards

What Every Student Receives

Six foundational pillars supporting your creative growth from day one

Industrial Tailoring Labs

Juki lockstitch, serger, and blind-hem machines alongside calibrated couture dress forms.

Botanical Dye & Loom Studio

Natural pigment vats, handlooms, and sustainable fabric sourcing workshops.

CLO 3D & Adobe CAD

Dedicated digital fashion workstations for virtual draping and technical flatpacks.

Annual Runway Showcase

Graduate runway production with lighting, casting, and visiting luxury house buyers.

On-Campus Lookbook Studio

Editorial photography cyclorama and styling closets for shooting your portfolio plates.

Industry Mentorship

Direct critique sessions with visiting couturiers, textile directors, and stylists.
