Tuition & Scholarship FAQ

How do I qualify for a portfolio merit scholarship?
All applicants who submit 8 to 12 creative pieces during registration are automatically reviewed by the Admissions Jury for merit grants ranging from 10% to 25% off semester tuition.
Are fabric and sewing supplies included in tuition?
Your starter drafting kit, muslin practice yardage, pattern paper, and industrial machine access are included. Specialty couture silks and embellishments for final runway looks are selected by each student.
